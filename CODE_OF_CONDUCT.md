# Community conduct

We want Kotodama's public collaboration spaces to be safe, constructive, and
useful.

- Be respectful and specific when discussing people, ideas, and code.
- Critique observable behavior and evidence rather than personal attributes.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, or publish another person's private
  information.
- Do not post secrets, private source material, participant data, audio, or
  transcripts.
- Follow maintainer requests that protect safety, privacy, and review quality.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when needed to enforce these expectations. Use GitHub's content
reporting and abuse-reporting controls for conduct matters, and do not place
private details in a public issue. `SECURITY.md` is reserved for software
vulnerabilities; the project does not yet offer a separate private conduct
inbox.
